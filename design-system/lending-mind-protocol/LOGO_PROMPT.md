# Logo generation prompt

Create an abstract, text-free identity mark for “Lending-Mind Protocol,” a Rust-first local runtime that turns engineering judgment into signed, inspectable policy and evidence. The mark should suggest a mind, a lending/transfer motion, and a guarded protocol boundary without drawing a literal brain, person, lock, shield, eye, or cloud.

Use a compact geometric symbol built from two or three interlocking structural forms: a central negative-space node, an asymmetric orbit or folded pathway around it, and one deliberate interruption that implies a policy gate. The silhouette must remain recognizable when reduced to a 24px app icon, favicon, or monochrome terminal glyph. It should feel authored, technical, calm, and durable rather than playful, futuristic, crypto-themed, or corporate stock art.

Visual specification: square canvas, centered mark, generous clear space, transparent background, no words, no letters, no numbers, no initials, no arrows, no gradients, no drop shadow, no mockup, no border, no watermark. Use near-black `#090a0b` and restrained emerald `#34d399` as the only colors, with a fully monochrome fallback in `#151718`. Use crisp vector-like edges, balanced optical weight, and a construction that survives both dark and light backgrounds. Export a clean high-resolution PNG with alpha plus an SVG-ready vector interpretation.
